ssh-analytics

A private, single-owner reporting tool for the developer's own YouTube channels.

What it does

ssh-analytics is a small set of scripts that runs on the channel owner's own computers. With read-only access granted by the owner, it reads channel and video statistics through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API, and turns them into daily and weekly performance reports (views, watch time, impressions, click-through rate) for the owner's channels.

Who can use it

The tool is not offered to the public. It is used only by its developer, who is also the owner of every channel it reads. It never posts, edits or deletes anything on YouTube.

Permissions requested

  • youtube.readonly: view the owner's channel and video details
  • yt-analytics.readonly: view the owner's YouTube Analytics reports
